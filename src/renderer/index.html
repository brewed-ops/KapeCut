Setup

KapeCut needs a few tools.

Install what is missing, then Re-check. KapeCut checks them on every launch.

    Guide

    Six steps to your first video.

    1. 01

      Open a folder with your brief and references: Open folder in the Projects menu, or drop the folder on the window.

    2. 02

      Connect your Agent: Connect your agent above the viewer copies the Connect prompt. Paste it into your Agent.

    3. 03

      Watch the Scenes arrive in Scenes as your Agent writes them, and play them in the viewer.

    4. 04

      Drag Scenes to reorder them. Pause on a Frame, press Note and click the picture.

    5. 05

      Copy the Fix prompt: Copy Prompt under Notes. Paste it into your Agent to fix them.

    6. 06

      Export the MP4 with Export at the top right.
